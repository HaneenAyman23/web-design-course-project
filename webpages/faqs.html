Setup & Installation

Setting up is easy:

  1. Download the Smart Home app
  2. Create an account
  3. Plug in your device
  4. Follow app instructions
  5. Connect to Wi-Fi

Most devices are ready in 5 minutes!

No special wiring needed! Just screw in smart bulbs like regular bulbs. Keep the light switch ON and control through the app.

Troubleshooting

Try these steps:

  1. Restart device and router
  2. Use 2.4GHz Wi-Fi (not 5GHz)
  3. Move closer to router
  4. Check password
  5. Factory reset device
