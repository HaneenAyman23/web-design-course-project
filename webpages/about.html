Our Journey in Smart Home Innovation

What started as a passion project in a garage has evolved into a leading force in smart home technology. Our founders, tech enthusiasts frustrated by complex, incompatible systems, set out to create a different kind of smart home company.

Today, Smart Home stands as a testament to what happens when innovation meets practicality. We've helped thousands of families experience the future of home living—today.
